# Category Playbook: HiFi Audio & Headphone Amplifiers (`hifi-audio`)

## 1. Stable Decision Physics & Core Trade-offs
- **Linearity & Signal-to-Noise**: SINAD (Signal-to-Noise and Distortion Ratio) and THD+N (Total Harmonic Distortion + Noise) measured via calibrated analyzer (Audio Precision APx555).
- **Impedance Matching & Damping Factor**: Output impedance (<1Ω for multi-BA IEMs to prevent frequency response skewing) vs high-voltage swing for 300Ω/600Ω high-impedance dynamic headphones.
- **Intermodulation Distortion (IMD)**: Multitone test behavior and jitter suppression on digital USB/SPDIF inputs.

## 2. Testing Conventions vs Review Anecdotes
- **Reject Subjective Review Stars**: Consumer star ratings and poetic marketing ("warm velvet mids") cannot substantiate acoustic superiority.
- **Instrumented Evidence Standard**: Prioritize independent APx555 test sweeps, 50mV SNR measurements (for ultra-sensitive IEM background hiss), and step response graphs.

## 3. Search & Regulatory Routing
- **Bluetooth Codec Licensing**: Live verify LDAC / aptX Adaptive certification on official Sony / Qualcomm portal.
- **Hi-Res Audio Certification**: `site:jas-audio.or.jp/english/hi-res-logo-member-list "{brand_or_model}"`
