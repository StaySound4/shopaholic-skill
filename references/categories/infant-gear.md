# Category Playbook: Infant Safety & Child Restraints (`infant-gear`)

## 1. Stable Decision Physics & Dynamic Crash Criteria
- **Crash Standards**: UN ECE R129 (i-Size) side-impact protection with Q-series biofidelic sensor dummies vs obsolete R44 standard.
- **Energy Absorption Materials**: Injection-molded EPP (Expanded Polypropylene) / EPS liners vs cheap blow-molded plastic shells.
- **Toxicological Safety**: GB 30007 / OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Class I (Infants) for skin-contact fabrics, forbidding formaldehyde, aryl amines, and plasticizers (phthalates).

## 2. Hard Exclusion & Inspection Rules
- **Non-Inspectable Hidden History Restriction**: Used / secondhand child car seats are strictly discouraged due to non-destructively undetectable internal micro-fractures from potential prior vehicle collisions.
- **Mandatory Domestic CCC**: `site:cqc.com.cn OR site:cnca.gov.cn "{model_name}"` (Mandatory China market certification).
