# Category Playbook: Coffee & Espresso Equipment (`coffee`)

## 1. Stable Decision Physics & Core Trade-offs
- **Thermodynamic Stability**: Single thermoblock vs dual thermoblock vs boiler with PID control. Thermal recovery time under back-to-back shots is the primary physical constraint within entry/mid-tier budgets.
- **Extraction Pressure & Flow**: 9-bar OPV calibration vs uncontrolled 15-bar vibration pump surge. Flow profiling needle valve vs fixed orifice.
- **Grinder Particle Distribution**: Bimodal (traditional espresso body) vs unimodal (modern high-extraction clarity). Burr alignment and retention.

## 2. Mandatory Safety & Material Compliance Anchors
- **Food Contact Safety (China)**: `GB 4806.1-2016` (General safety), `GB 4806.9-2016` (Metal contact / lead/nickel leaching), `GB 4806.7-2016` (Silicone group head gaskets and tubing).
- **Portal Search Anchor**: `site:std.samr.gov.cn "GB 4806"`
- **Electrical Safety**: Mandatory CCC for pressurized boiler appliances (`GB 4706.1`, `GB 4706.19`).

## 3. Evidence Role & Testing Conventions
- **No Universal BOM Teardown Quota**: Food/extraction equipment prioritizes water temperature stability logging and heavy metal leaching compliance rather than silicon chip teardowns.
- **Volatile Fact Verification**: Live check current pricing and localized warranty via official retailer portal rather than hardcoded static price claims.
