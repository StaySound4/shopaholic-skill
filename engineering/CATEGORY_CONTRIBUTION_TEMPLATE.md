## Change-control checklist

- [ ] No volatile fact is presented as timeless without a date/verification rule.
- [ ] No company/brand source is treated as independent measurement.
- [ ] No anecdote count is converted into prevalence without denominator support.
- [ ] No origin claim is inferred from company nationality or barcode prefix alone.
- [ ] No universal numeric threshold is included without scope/test-condition/evidence.
- [ ] The playbook can be loaded independently without requiring a chain of nested references.
